    26→## 1. Tong quan kien truc Webhook
    27→
    28→### 1.1 Luong xu ly hien tai
    29→
    30→```
    31→Platform (FB/Zalo/TG/...)
    32→  -> POST /api/webhook/[channel]
    33→    -> proxy.ts: BO qua auth/CSRF/rate-limit (public path)
    34→    -> route.ts: Doc rawBody, tai ChannelConfig tu DB
    35→    -> channelRegistry.verifyWebhook(channel, rawBody, headers, config)
    36→      -> adapter.verifyWebhook() -- HMAC-SHA256 hoac platform-specific
    37→    -> channelRegistry.handleWebhook(channel, payload, channel)
    38→      -> adapter.handleWebhook() -- Tra ve ParsedWebhookMessage[]
    39→    -> Tra ve { received: N, messages: [...] } nhu JSON response
    40→```
    41→
    42→### 1.2 Endpoint chung
    43→
    44→| Phuong thuc | URL | Mo ta |
    45→|---|---|---|
    46→| `POST` | `/api/webhook/{channel}` | Nhan webhook tu platform |
    47→| `GET` | `/api/channels` | Lay danh sach channels + config |
    48→| `PUT` | `/api/channels` | Cap nhat channel config |
    49→
    50→### 1.3 Danh sach channel va header verification
    51→
    52→| Channel | URL Path | Signature Header | Thuat toan |
    53→|---|---|---|---|
    54→| Facebook Messenger | `/api/webhook/facebook_messenger` | `X-Hub-Signature-256` | HMAC-SHA256(appSecret, rawBody) |
    55→| Facebook Comment | `/api/webhook/facebook_comment` | `X-Hub-Signature-256` | HMAC-SHA256(appSecret, rawBody) |
    56→| Zalo OA | `/api/webhook/zalo` | `X-Zalo-Signature` | HMAC-SHA256(appSecret, rawBody) |
    57→| Telegram Bot | `/api/webhook/telegram` | (trong body: `secret_token`) | So sanh secret_token trong payload |
    58→| Chatwork | `/api/webhook/chatwork` | `X-ChatWorkWebhookSignature` | HMAC-SHA256(apiToken, rawBody) |
    59→| Website Widget | `/api/webhook/website` | `X-Webhook-Signature` + `X-Webhook-Timestamp` | HMAC-SHA256(secret, timestamp.rawBody) |
    60→| Email | `/api/webhook/email` | (Khong co) | Luon pass (khong verify) |
    61→
    62→### 1.4 Cau truc response webhook
    63→
    64→```json
    65→{
    66→  "received": 1,
    67→  "messages": [
    68→    {
    69→      "platform": "facebook_messenger",
    70→      "platformMessageId": "m_xxxxx",

    78→    }
    79→  ]
    80→}
    81→```
